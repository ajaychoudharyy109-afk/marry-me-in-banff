# Editing your website — a quick guide for Lorelle

You can change your own website's words, photos, prices and layout whenever you like. No code, nothing to install.

## Signing in

1. Go to **marrymeinbanff.ca/admin**
2. Click **Login with GitHub** (the first time, click **Authorize** when GitHub asks).
3. On the left, click **Website**. You'll see two things:
   - **Home page — sections, photos & wording** — everything on your page
   - **Site settings** — logo, the top-right button, footer, Instagram, email, colours

## Changing words

1. Open **Home page**. Your page is a list of blocks, from top to bottom, just like the real page.
2. Click a block to open it. Change the words.
3. Click **Publish → Publish now** (top right).
4. Wait about a minute, then refresh your website. That's it.

**Titles have two parts:** the plain words, and the *coloured italic ending*. Fill in both and they join up on the page.

Typing **Marry Me, In Banff** in any paragraph automatically gives it the pretty italic brand colour.

## Changing a photo

Click the photo box in that block → **Choose different image** → **Upload** → pick a photo from your computer or phone (JPG or PNG).

- Big photos are fine. They're shrunk and straightened for you.
- Always fill in **Photo description** (e.g. "Veil catching the wind above the Fairmont"). It helps Google and anyone using a screen reader.

## The floating gallery

Open the **Floating photo gallery** block → **Photos**.
- **Add Photo** to add one. Remove one with its bin icon.
- Drag a photo up or down to change the order it floats in.

## Packages and prices

Open the **Packages** block → click a package → change **Price**, the text, or the "What's included" list. Use the bullet button for lists and **H4** for the small coloured sub-headings.

## Moving things around

Each block can be **dragged up or down** (grab it by its title bar) to change the order on the page. The menu at the top of the site follows along automatically.

## Adding a new section

At the bottom of the list click **Add Section** and choose a type. **Text section (new)** is a plain heading-and-paragraphs area, great for anything that doesn't fit elsewhere.

## The menu at the top

Each block has a **Menu label**. Type a word to show it in the menu; clear it to hide it.

## A few friendly rules

- **Publish goes live straight away** (after about a minute) — there's no preview. Give the page a quick look afterwards.
- Not sure about deleting a block or changing colours? Ask AJ first. Everything can be undone, so nothing is ever lost.
- If something looks wrong after you publish, don't worry — message AJ and it can be put back in a click.
- Photo from an iPhone not uploading? It's usually a "HEIC" file. Take a screenshot of it or share it as a JPG, or tell AJ.

Happy editing! ❖
